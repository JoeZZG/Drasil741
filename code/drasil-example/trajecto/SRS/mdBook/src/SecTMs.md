## Lorentz force law {#TM:lorentzForce}

</div>

|Refname    |TM:lorentzForce                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
|:----------|:---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|Label      |Lorentz force law                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
|Equation   |\\[\boldsymbol{F}=q\\,\left(\boldsymbol{E}+\boldsymbol{v}\text{×}\boldsymbol{B}\right)\\]                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
|Description|<ul><li>\\(\boldsymbol{F}\\) is the force (\\({\text{N}}\\))</li><li>\\(q\\) is the particle charge (\\({\text{C}}\\))</li><li>\\(\boldsymbol{E}\\) is the electric field vector (\\(\frac{\text{N}}{\text{C}}\\))</li><li>\\(\boldsymbol{v}\text{×}\boldsymbol{B}\\) is the cross product of velocity and magnetic field (Unitless)</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
|Notes      |<ul><li>The electromagnetic force on a charged particle is F = q*(E + v×B), where \\(\boldsymbol{F}\\) is the total force vector, \\(q\\) is the particle charge, \\(\boldsymbol{E}\\) is the electric field vector, and \\(\boldsymbol{v}\text{×}\boldsymbol{B}\\) is the cross product of velocity and magnetic flux density.. For 2D planar motion with an out-of-plane magnetic field, this reduces to the component equations derived in the general definitions. Assumes [A:singleParticle](./SecAssumps.md#singleParticle), [A:prescribedFields](./SecAssumps.md#prescribedFields).</li></ul>|
|Source     |--                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

</div>

|Refname    |TM:eqnMotion                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

|Label      |Equation of motion under electromagnetic fields                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
|Equation   |\\[{a\_{\text{x}}}=\frac{q}{m}\\,\left({E\_{\text{x}}}+{v\_{\text{y}}}\\,B\right)\\]\\[{a\_{\text{y}}}=\frac{q}{m}\\,\left({E\_{\text{y}}}-{v\_{\text{x}}}\\,B\right)\\]                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
|Description|<ul><li>\\({a\_{\text{x}}}\\) is the x-acceleration of the particle (\\(\frac{\text{m}}{\text{s}^{2}}\\))</li><li>\\(q\\) is the particle charge (\\({\text{C}}\\))</li><li>\\(m\\) is the particle mass (\\({\text{kg}}\\))</li><li>\\({E\_{\text{x}}}\\) is the x-component of the electric field (\\(\frac{\text{N}}{\text{C}}\\))</li><li>\\({v\_{\text{y}}}\\) is the y-velocity of the particle (\\(\frac{\text{m}}{\text{s}}\\))</li><li>\\(B\\) is the out-of-plane magnetic flux density (\\({\text{T}}\\))</li></ul><ul><li>\\({a\_{\text{y}}}\\) is the y-acceleration of the particle (\\(\frac{\text{m}}{\text{s}^{2}}\\))</li><li>\\(q\\) is the particle charge (\\({\text{C}}\\))</li><li>\\(m\\) is the particle mass (\\({\text{kg}}\\))</li><li>\\({E\_{\text{y}}}\\) is the y-component of the electric field (\\(\frac{\text{N}}{\text{C}}\\))</li><li>\\({v\_{\text{x}}}\\) is the x-velocity of the particle (\\(\frac{\text{m}}{\text{s}}\\))</li><li>\\(B\\) is the out-of-plane magnetic flux density (\\({\text{T}}\\))</li></ul>|
|Notes      |<ul><li>Combining Newton's second law with the Lorentz force law, ([TM:lorentzForce](./SecTMs.md#TM:lorentzForce)), the equations of motion for each velocity component are obtained. The quantity q/m is the charge-to-mass ratio. Assumes [A:singleParticle](./SecAssumps.md#singleParticle), [A:prescribedFields](./SecAssumps.md#prescribedFields), [A:lorentzOnly](./SecAssumps.md#lorentzOnly).</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
