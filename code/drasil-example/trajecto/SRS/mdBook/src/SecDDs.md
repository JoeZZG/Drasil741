# Data Definitions {#Sec:DDs}

This section collects and defines all the data needed to build the instance models.

<div align="center">

## Charge-to-mass ratio {#DD:qOverm}

</div>

|Refname    |DD:qOverm                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
|:----------|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|Label      |Charge-to-mass ratio                                                                                                                                                                                       |
|Symbol     |\\(κ\\)                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
|Units      |\\(\frac{\text{C}}{\text{kg}}\\)                                                                                                                                                                           |
|Equation   |\\[κ=\frac{q}{m}\\]                                                                                                                                                                                        |
|Description|<ul><li>\\(κ\\) is the charge-to-mass ratio (\\(\frac{\text{C}}{\text{kg}}\\))</li><li>\\(q\\) is the particle charge (\\({\text{C}}\\))</li><li>\\(m\\) is the particle mass (\\({\text{kg}}\\))</li></ul>|
|Notes      |<ul><li>\\(q\\) is the particle charge and \\(m\\) is the particle mass. The ratio \\(κ\\) is used to simplify the equations of motion..</li></ul>                                                         |
|Source     |--                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
|RefBy      |[IM:stateEvol](./SecIMs.md#IM:stateEvol) and [GD:dyn2D](./SecGDs.md#GD:dyn2D)                                                                                                                              |

|Notes      |<ul><li>\\({x\_{0}}\\), \\({y\_{0}}\\) are the initial position components (m) and \\({v\_{\text{x}0}}\\), \\({v\_{\text{y}0}}\\) are the initial velocity components (m/s)..</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

## Electric field vector {#DD:eField}

</div>

|Refname    |DD:eField                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
|:----------|:------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|Label      |Electric field vector                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
|Symbol     |\\(\boldsymbol{E}\\)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
|Units      |\\(\frac{\text{N}}{\text{C}}\\)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
|Equation   |\\[\boldsymbol{E}=\begin{bmatrix}{E\_{\text{x}}} & {E\_{\text{y}}} & 0\end{bmatrix}\\]                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
|Description|<ul><li>\\(\boldsymbol{E}\\) is the electric field vector (\\(\frac{\text{N}}{\text{C}}\\))</li><li>\\({E\_{\text{x}}}\\) is the x-component of the electric field (\\(\frac{\text{N}}{\text{C}}\\))</li><li>\\({E\_{\text{y}}}\\) is the y-component of the electric field (\\(\frac{\text{N}}{\text{C}}\\))</li></ul>                    |
|Notes      |<ul><li>\\(\boldsymbol{E}\\) is the electric field vector. \\({E\_{\text{x}}}\\) and \\({E\_{\text{y}}}\\) are the in-plane components (N/C). The out-of-plane component is zero by [A:eAxisAligned](./SecAssumps.md#eAxisAligned) and the fields are user-specified per. [A:prescribedFields](./SecAssumps.md#prescribedFields).</li></ul>|
|Source     |--                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
|RefBy      |[DD:fieldsByRegion](./SecDDs.md#DD:fieldsByRegion)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |

|Notes      |<ul><li>\\(\boldsymbol{B}\\) is the magnetic flux density vector. \\(B\\) is the out-of-plane (z) component (T). The in-plane components are zero by [A:bPerpPlane](./SecAssumps.md#bPerpPlane) ; fields are fixed by. [A:prescribedFields](./SecAssumps.md#prescribedFields).</li></ul>|

## Rectangular field region {#DD:regionRect}

</div>

|Refname    |DD:regionRect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
|:----------|:-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|Label      |Rectangular field region                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
|Symbol     |\\({R\_{i}}\\)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
|Units      |Unitless                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
|Equation   |\\[{R\_{i}}=\begin{bmatrix}{x\_{0}} & {x\_{\text{det}}} & {{y\_{\text{min}}}^{\text{det}}} & {{y\_{\text{max}}}^{\text{det}}}\end{bmatrix}\\]                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
|Description|<ul><li>\\({R\_{i}}\\) is the rectangular field region (Unitless)</li><li>\\({x\_{0}}\\) is the initial x-position (\\({\text{m}}\\))</li><li>\\({x\_{\text{det}}}\\) is the detector line x-position (\\({\text{m}}\\))</li><li>\\({{y\_{\text{min}}}^{\text{det}}}\\) is the minimum y-coordinate of detector (\\({\text{m}}\\))</li><li>\\({{y\_{\text{max}}}^{\text{det}}}\\) is the maximum y-coordinate of detector (\\({\text{m}}\\))</li></ul>|
|Notes      |<ul><li>Each field region is defined as the Cartesian product of two intervals: R_i = [x_min(i), x_max(i)] x [y_min(i), y_max(i)], where x_min(i), x_max(i), y_min(i), y_max(i) are the axis-aligned rectangular boundary coordinates (all in m), per [A:rectRegions](./SecAssumps.md#rectRegions)..</li></ul>                                                                                                                                        |
|Source     |--                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
|RefBy      |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

</div>

|Refname    |DD:fieldsByRegion                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
|:----------|:------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|Label      |Electric field in region i                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
|Symbol     |\\({\boldsymbol{E}\_{i}}\\)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
|Units      |\\(\frac{\text{N}}{\text{C}}\\)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
|Equation   |\\[{\boldsymbol{E}\_{i}}=\boldsymbol{E}\\]                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
|Description|<ul><li>\\({\boldsymbol{E}\_{i}}\\) is the electric field in region i (\\(\frac{\text{N}}{\text{C}}\\))</li><li>\\(\boldsymbol{E}\\) is the electric field vector (\\(\frac{\text{N}}{\text{C}}\\))</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
|Notes      |<ul><li>The electric field \\({\boldsymbol{E}\_{i}}\\) and the magnetic flux density \\({\boldsymbol{B}\_{i}}\\) are constant within each region R_i: E(r) = E_i and B(r) = B_i for all r in R_i, per [A:piecewiseUniform](./SecAssumps.md#piecewiseUniform). The fields may differ between regions. \\({\boldsymbol{E}\_{i}}\\) depends on [DD:eField](./SecDDs.md#DD:eField) and. \\({\boldsymbol{B}\_{i}}\\) depends on [DD:bField](./SecDDs.md#DD:bField)..</li></ul>|
|Source     |--                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

## Detector line {#DD:detectorLine}

</div>

|Refname    |DD:detectorLine                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
|:----------|:-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|Label      |Detector line                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
|Symbol     |\\({L\_{\text{det}}}\\)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
|Units      |Unitless                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
|Equation   |\\[{L\_{\text{det}}}=\begin{bmatrix}{x\_{\text{det}}} & {{y\_{\text{min}}}^{\text{det}}} & {{y\_{\text{max}}}^{\text{det}}}\end{bmatrix}\\]                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
|Description|<ul><li>\\({L\_{\text{det}}}\\) is the detector line (Unitless)</li><li>\\({x\_{\text{det}}}\\) is the detector line x-position (\\({\text{m}}\\))</li><li>\\({{y\_{\text{min}}}^{\text{det}}}\\) is the minimum y-coordinate of detector (\\({\text{m}}\\))</li><li>\\({{y\_{\text{max}}}^{\text{det}}}\\) is the maximum y-coordinate of detector (\\({\text{m}}\\))</li></ul>                                                                              |
|Notes      |<ul><li>\\({L\_{\text{det}}}\\) is the detector line segment. It is defined as the set of points L_det = the set of (x,y) where x = x_det and y in [y_min(det), y_max(det)], per [A:lineDetector](./SecAssumps.md#lineDetector). where \\({x\_{\text{det}}}\\) is the x-coordinate of the detector, and \\({{y\_{\text{min}}}^{\text{det}}}\\), \\({{y\_{\text{max}}}^{\text{det}}}\\) are the lower and upper y-bounds of the detector (all in m)..</li></ul>|
|Source     |--                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
|RefBy      |[IM:detHit](./SecIMs.md#IM:detHit)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
