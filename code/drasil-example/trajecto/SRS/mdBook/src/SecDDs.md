## Rectangular field region {#DD:regionRect}

</div>

|Refname    |DD:regionRect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
|:----------|:-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|Label      |Rectangular field region                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
|Symbol     |\\({R\_{i}}\\)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
|Units      |Unitless                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
|Equation   |\\[{R\_{i}}=\begin{bmatrix}{x\_{\text{grid}}} & {y\_{\text{grid}}} & w & h\end{bmatrix}\\]                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
|Description|<ul><li>\\({R\_{i}}\\) is the rectangular field region (Unitless)</li><li>\\({x\_{\text{grid}}}\\) is the grid origin x-coordinate (\\({\text{m}}\\))</li><li>\\({y\_{\text{grid}}}\\) is the grid origin y-coordinate (\\({\text{m}}\\))</li><li>\\(w\\) is the region width (\\({\text{m}}\\))</li><li>\\(h\\) is the region height (\\({\text{m}}\\))</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
|Notes      |<ul><li>Each field region R_i (for i = 1 .. \\(N\\) ) is an axis-aligned rectangle of width \\(w\\) and height \\(h\\). The equation above lists the grid parameters that define each region. For a row layout (regions tiled left-to-right): R_i = [x_grid + (i-1)*w, x_grid + i*w] x [y_grid, y_grid + h]. For a column layout (regions tiled bottom-to-top): R_i = [x_grid, x_grid + w] x [y_grid + (i-1)*h, y_grid + i*h]. The grid origin (\\({x\_{\text{grid}}}\\), \\({y\_{\text{grid}}}\\)) anchors region 1, per [A:rectRegions](./SecAssumps.md#rectRegions) and [A:gridLayout](./SecAssumps.md#gridLayout).</li></ul>|
|Source     |--                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
|RefBy      |[FR:Build-Region-Grid](./SecFRs.md#buildRegionGrid) and [DD:fieldsByRegion](./SecDDs.md#DD:fieldsByRegion)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

|Label      |Electric field in region i                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
|Symbol     |\\({\boldsymbol{E}\_{i}}\\)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
|Units      |\\(\frac{\text{N}}{\text{C}}\\)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
|Equation   |\\[{\boldsymbol{E}\_{i}}=\begin{bmatrix}{E\_{\text{x}}} & {E\_{\text{y}}} & B\end{bmatrix}\\]                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
|Description|<ul><li>\\({\boldsymbol{E}\_{i}}\\) is the electric field in region i (\\(\frac{\text{N}}{\text{C}}\\))</li><li>\\({E\_{\text{x}}}\\) is the x-component of the electric field (\\(\frac{\text{N}}{\text{C}}\\))</li><li>\\({E\_{\text{y}}}\\) is the y-component of the electric field (\\(\frac{\text{N}}{\text{C}}\\))</li><li>\\(B\\) is the out-of-plane magnetic flux density (\\({\text{T}}\\))</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
|Notes      |<ul><li>Each region R_i has its own independently specified electric field \\({\boldsymbol{E}\_{i}}\\) = (Ex_i, Ey_i, 0) and magnetic flux density \\({\boldsymbol{B}\_{i}}\\) = (0, 0, B_i). The fields are constant within each region per [A:piecewiseUniform](./SecAssumps.md#piecewiseUniform). The active fields at time t are determined by which region R_i ([DD:regionRect](./SecDDs.md#DD:regionRect)) contains the particle position (x(t), y(t)). If the particle is outside all defined regions, the fields default to zero and the particle undergoes free (force-free) motion. The per-region values (Ex_i, Ey_i, B_i) are user-specified inputs per [A:prescribedFields](./SecAssumps.md#prescribedFields).</li></ul>|
|Source     |--                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |

## Detector line {#DD:detectorLine}

</div>

|Refname    |DD:detectorLine                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
|:----------|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|Label      |Detector line                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
|Symbol     |\\({L\_{\text{det}}}\\)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
|Units      |Unitless                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
|Equation   |\\[{L\_{\text{det}}}=\begin{bmatrix}{d\_{\text{orient}}} & {x\_{\text{det}}} & {y\_{\text{det}}} & {{y\_{\text{min}}}^{\text{det}}} & {{y\_{\text{max}}}^{\text{det}}} & {{x\_{\text{min}}}^{\text{det}}} & {{x\_{\text{max}}}^{\text{det}}}\end{bmatrix}\\]                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
|Description|<ul><li>\\({L\_{\text{det}}}\\) is the detector line (Unitless)</li><li>\\({d\_{\text{orient}}}\\) is the detector orientation (Unitless)</li><li>\\({x\_{\text{det}}}\\) is the detector line x-position (\\({\text{m}}\\))</li><li>\\({y\_{\text{det}}}\\) is the detector line y-position (\\({\text{m}}\\))</li><li>\\({{y\_{\text{min}}}^{\text{det}}}\\) is the minimum y-coordinate of detector (\\({\text{m}}\\))</li><li>\\({{y\_{\text{max}}}^{\text{det}}}\\) is the maximum y-coordinate of detector (\\({\text{m}}\\))</li><li>\\({{x\_{\text{min}}}^{\text{det}}}\\) is the minimum x-coordinate of detector (\\({\text{m}}\\))</li><li>\\({{x\_{\text{max}}}^{\text{det}}}\\) is the maximum x-coordinate of detector (\\({\text{m}}\\))</li></ul>|
|Notes      |<ul><li>\\({L\_{\text{det}}}\\) is the detector line segment. When \\({d\_{\text{orient}}}\\) = 0 (vertical), the detector is L_det is the set of (x,y) such that x = x_det and y in [y_min(det), y_max(det)]. When \\({d\_{\text{orient}}}\\) = 1 (horizontal), the detector is L_det is the set of (x,y) such that y = y_det and x in [x_min(det), x_max(det)]. The detector orientation and bounds are user-specified, per [A:lineDetector](./SecAssumps.md#lineDetector).</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
|Source     |--                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
