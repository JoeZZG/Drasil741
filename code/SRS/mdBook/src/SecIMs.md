# Instance Models {#Sec:IMs}

This section transforms the problem defined in the [problem description](./SecProbDesc.md#Sec:ProbDesc) into one which is expressed in mathematical terms. It uses concrete symbols defined in the [data definitions](./SecDDs.md#Sec:DDs) to replace the abstract symbols in the models identified in [theoretical models](./SecTMs.md#Sec:TMs) and [general definitions](./SecGDs.md#Sec:GDs).

|Description       |<ul><li>\\(x\\) is the x-position of the particle (\\({\text{m}}\\))</li><li>\\({x\_{\text{det}}}\\) is the detector line x-position (\\({\text{m}}\\))</li><li>\\({y\_{\text{hit}}}\\) is the y-coordinate of impact point (\\({\text{m}}\\))</li><li>\\({y\_{\text{detMin}}}\\) is the minimum y-coordinate of detector (\\({\text{m}}\\))</li><li>\\({y\_{\text{detMax}}}\\) is the maximum y-coordinate of detector (\\({\text{m}}\\))</li><li>\\(y\\) is the y-position of the particle (\\({\text{m}}\\))</li><li>\\({y\_{\text{det}}}\\) is the detector line y-position (\\({\text{m}}\\))</li><li>\\({x\_{\text{hit}}}\\) is the x-coordinate of impact point (\\({\text{m}}\\))</li><li>\\({x\_{\text{detMin}}}\\) is the minimum x-coordinate of detector (\\({\text{m}}\\))</li><li>\\({x\_{\text{detMax}}}\\) is the maximum x-coordinate of detector (\\({\text{m}}\\))</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
|Notes             |<ul><li>The detector is modelled as a line segment that may be vertical or horizontal, selected by the orientation flag \\({d\_{\text{orient}}}\\) ([DD:detectorLine](./SecDDs.md#DD:detectorLine)). Vertical case (D = 0): the detector is at x = \\({x\_{\text{det}}}\\) with y-bounds [\\({y\_{\text{detMin}}}\\), \\({y\_{\text{detMax}}}\\)]. The hit condition is x(t) = x_det and y(t) in [y_min, y_max]. Horizontal case (D = 1): the detector is at y = \\({y\_{\text{det}}}\\) with x-bounds [\\({x\_{\text{detMin}}}\\), \\({x\_{\text{detMax}}}\\)]. The hit condition is y(t) = y_det and x(t) in [x_min, x_max]. In both cases, \\({t\_{\text{hit}}}\\) is the minimum t in [0, \\({t\_{\text{final}}}\\)] satisfying the condition. If no such t exists, \\({t\_{\text{hit}}}\\) is set to -1 as a sentinel value indicating no hit, and the hit coordinates are undefined. The trajectory (x(t), y(t)) is provided by [IM:stateEvol](./SecIMs.md#IM:stateEvol). Applicable assumptions: [A:fullDetection](./SecAssumps.md#fullDetection) and [A:lineDetector](./SecAssumps.md#lineDetector).</li></ul>|
