# Instance Models {#Sec:IMs}

This section transforms the problem defined in the [problem description](./SecProbDesc.md#Sec:ProbDesc) into one which is expressed in mathematical terms. It uses concrete symbols defined in the [data definitions](./SecDDs.md#Sec:DDs) to replace the abstract symbols in the models identified in [theoretical models](./SecTMs.md#Sec:TMs) and [general definitions](./SecGDs.md#Sec:GDs).

|Description       |<ul><li>\\({t\_{\text{hit}}}\\) is the time of detector hit (\\({\text{s}}\\))</li><li>\\({t\_{\text{final}}}\\) is the final simulation time (\\({\text{s}}\\))</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
|Notes             |<ul><li>The detector is modelled as a vertical line segment at \\({x\_{\text{det}}}\\) with y-bounds \\({{y\_{\text{min}}}^{\text{det}}}\\) and \\({{y\_{\text{max}}}^{\text{det}}}\\) ([DD:detectorLine](./SecDDs.md#DD:detectorLine)). The hit time is defined as: t_hit = min t in [0, t_final] such that x(t) = x_det and y(t) in [y_min(det), y_max(det)]. The hit location is: x_hit = x_det, and y_hit = y(t_hit), \\({y\_{\text{hit}}}\\). If no such t exists within [0, t_final], then no detector hit occurs. The trajectory (x(t), y(t)) is provided by [IM:stateEvol](./SecIMs.md#IM:stateEvol). Applicable assumptions: [A:fullDetection](./SecAssumps.md#fullDetection) and. [A:lineDetector](./SecAssumps.md#lineDetector).</li></ul>|
