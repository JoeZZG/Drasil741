## X-acceleration under electromagnetic force {#GD:xAccelEM}

</div>

|Refname    |GD:xAccelEM                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
|:----------|:-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|Label      |X-acceleration under electromagnetic force                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
|Units      |\\(\frac{\text{m}}{\text{s}^{2}}\\)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
|Equation   |\\[{a\_{\text{x}}}=κ\\,\left({E\_{\text{x}}}+{v\_{\text{y}}}\right)\\,B\\]                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
|Description|<ul><li>\\({a\_{\text{x}}}\\) is the x-acceleration of the particle (\\(\frac{\text{m}}{\text{s}^{2}}\\))</li><li>\\(κ\\) is the charge-to-mass ratio (\\(\frac{\text{C}}{\text{kg}}\\))</li><li>\\({E\_{\text{x}}}\\) is the x-component of the electric field (\\(\frac{\text{N}}{\text{C}}\\))</li><li>\\({v\_{\text{y}}}\\) is the y-velocity of the particle (\\(\frac{\text{m}}{\text{s}}\\))</li><li>\\(B\\) is the out-of-plane magnetic flux density (\\({\text{T}}\\))</li></ul>|
|Notes      |<ul><li>Derived from the equation of motion by applying planar motion ([A:twoDMotion](./SecAssumps.md#twoDMotion)), out-of-plane B field ([A:bPerpPlane](./SecAssumps.md#bPerpPlane)), axis-aligned E field ([A:eAxisAligned](./SecAssumps.md#eAxisAligned)), and fixed fields ([A:prescribedFields](./SecAssumps.md#prescribedFields)). Here \\(κ\\) = q/m is defined in [DD:qOverm](./SecDDs.md#DD:qOverm).</li></ul>                                                                   |
