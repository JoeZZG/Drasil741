## Cross product v x B for out-of-plane magnetic field {#GD:vCrossB2D}

</div>

|Refname    |GD:vCrossB2D                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
|:----------|:-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|Label      |Cross product v x B for out-of-plane magnetic field                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
|Equation   |\\[\boldsymbol{v}\text{×}\boldsymbol{B}=\begin{bmatrix}{v\_{\text{y}}}\\,B & -{v\_{\text{x}}}\\,B & 0\end{bmatrix}\\]                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
|Description|<ul><li>\\(\boldsymbol{v}\text{×}\boldsymbol{B}\\) is the cross product of velocity and magnetic field (Unitless)</li><li>\\({v\_{\text{y}}}\\) is the y-velocity of the particle (\\(\frac{\text{m}}{\text{s}}\\))</li><li>\\(B\\) is the out-of-plane magnetic flux density (\\({\text{T}}\\))</li><li>\\({v\_{\text{x}}}\\) is the x-velocity of the particle (\\(\frac{\text{m}}{\text{s}}\\))</li></ul>|
|Notes      |<ul><li>Under planar motion ([A:twoDMotion](./SecAssumps.md#twoDMotion)) and an out-of-plane magnetic field ([A:bPerpPlane](./SecAssumps.md#bPerpPlane)), the velocity has the form v(t) = (vx(t), vy(t), 0) and the magnetic field has the form B = (0, 0, B). The cross product then reduces to the stated component form..</li></ul>                                                                     |

## Two-dimensional equations of motion under E and B {#GD:dyn2D}

</div>

|Refname    |GD:dyn2D                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
|:----------|:-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|Label      |Two-dimensional equations of motion under E and B                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
|Units      |\\(\frac{\text{m}}{\text{s}^{2}}\\)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
|Equation   |\\[\frac{\\,d{v\_{\text{x}}}}{\\,dt}=κ\\,\left({E\_{\text{x}}}+{v\_{\text{y}}}\\,B\right)\land{}\frac{\\,d{v\_{\text{y}}}}{\\,dt}=κ\\,\left({E\_{\text{y}}}-{v\_{\text{x}}}\\,B\right)\\]                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
|Description|<ul><li>\\(t\\) is the time (\\({\text{s}}\\))</li><li>\\({v\_{\text{x}}}\\) is the x-velocity of the particle (\\(\frac{\text{m}}{\text{s}}\\))</li><li>\\(κ\\) is the charge-to-mass ratio (\\(\frac{\text{C}}{\text{kg}}\\))</li><li>\\({E\_{\text{x}}}\\) is the x-component of the electric field (\\(\frac{\text{N}}{\text{C}}\\))</li><li>\\({v\_{\text{y}}}\\) is the y-velocity of the particle (\\(\frac{\text{m}}{\text{s}}\\))</li><li>\\(B\\) is the out-of-plane magnetic flux density (\\({\text{T}}\\))</li><li>\\({E\_{\text{y}}}\\) is the y-component of the electric field (\\(\frac{\text{N}}{\text{C}}\\))</li></ul>|
|Notes      |<ul><li>Assuming planar motion ([A:twoDMotion](./SecAssumps.md#twoDMotion)), prescribed fields ([A:prescribedFields](./SecAssumps.md#prescribedFields)), out-of-plane magnetic field ([A:bPerpPlane](./SecAssumps.md#bPerpPlane)), and axis-aligned electric field ([A:eAxisAligned](./SecAssumps.md#eAxisAligned)). Here \\(κ\\) = q/m is the charge-to-mass ratio defined in [DD:qOverm](./SecDDs.md#DD:qOverm) .. The equations are obtained from [TM:lorentzForce](./SecTMs.md#TM:lorentzForce) and [TM:eqnMotion](./SecTMs.md#TM:eqnMotion) by applying [GD:vCrossB2D](./SecGDs.md#GD:vCrossB2D)..</li></ul>                         |
